# Telegram LeadOps — 7-day pilot

Connect one approved Telegram Business workflow, analyze inbound conversations, rank likely buying opportunities, and provide a human-approved next action.

Deliverables:
- 100-message evaluation report
- AI vs baseline quality comparison
- redacted hot-lead examples
- response-priority workflow
- observed ROI report

No autonomous outbound messaging during the pilot.

Commercial test: obtain one paid pilot before adding broad CRM features.
