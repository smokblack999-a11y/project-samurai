# First paid pilot

Offer: connect one Telegram inbox, rank incoming conversations by buying intent, and produce a daily human follow-up queue.

Pilot outcome:
- baseline response time
- number of qualified conversations
- number of high-priority conversations
- follow-up completion
- customer-reported opportunities recovered

Do not sell "AI" as the outcome. Sell faster identification of conversations worth a human response.

Funnel target:
10 qualified prospects -> 5 demos -> 3 pilots -> 1 paid pilot.

If the funnel fails at the first stage, change targeting/offer before adding features.
