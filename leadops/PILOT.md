# Paid Pilot Gate

Target funnel: 10 qualified prospects -> 5 demos -> 3 pilots -> 1 paid pilot.

## Offer
Connect a Telegram inbox, classify incoming conversations, rank buying intent, and show the next human action. Measure response-time and qualified-lead changes against a baseline.

## Do not build before validation
No autonomous outbound messaging, no custom model, no large CRM, no Kubernetes, no mobile app.

## Kill rule
If 10 qualified prospects yield zero serious pilot conversations, stop feature work and change the ICP or offer.
