# First-Pilot Metric Contract

Track these fields for every pilot run:

- total_messages
- labeled_messages
- buying_precision
- buying_recall
- buying_f1
- false_positive_rate
- median_latency_ms
- p95_latency_ms
- ai_cost_per_message
- qualified_leads
- human_followup_rate
- paid_pilot_value

Commercial decision rule:

Do not claim product-market fit from synthetic data. A pilot becomes sales evidence only after at least 100 real labeled messages and one paid customer.
