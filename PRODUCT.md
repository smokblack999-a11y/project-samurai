# Revenue-first MVP

## ICP

Small sales/support teams already receiving meaningful Telegram conversations and losing leads in scattered chats.

## Pain

Slow response, no lead pipeline, no ownership, no searchable history, inconsistent replies.

## Offer

Telegram Sales Console: unified inbox + lead pipeline + AI reply drafts + basic analytics.

## Pilot

$500 setup + $99/month. First 3 customers receive implementation support and a fixed pilot scope.

## Success gates

1. 3 qualified prospects interviewed.
2. 1 paid pilot.
3. 50+ real conversations processed.
4. Median operator response time improves.
5. At least one measurable lead/conversion improvement.

## Kill criteria

Do not expand into a large multi-account platform before a paid pilot. If 30 qualified outreach conversations produce no serious interest, change the ICP/offer before adding features.
